Which transform mode should I use?

  • Affine is best suited for two scans that are both basically flat and square-on - it can correct shifting, rotation, scaling, and stretching.
  • Perspective is best suited when a page was photographed at an angle, or a book wasn't lying fully flat - it corrects everything Affine does, plus perspective distortion.
  • Thin Plate Spline is best suited for a page with local warping or curvature (e.g. from the gutter of a bound book) - it corrects everything Perspective does, plus uneven, non-linear bending.

Colors for the Superimposed view

Ink that is only in copy 1 is shown in the first color, ink that is only in copy 2 in the second. Ink that is in both turns out dark.
