    1. 1

      Two views of one project. Compare and Pages are tabs. Switch at any time; nothing is lost and there is no Back button. The project saves itself.

    2. 2

      The two copies. Copy 1 has a menu because it rarely changes. Copy 2 has step buttons. "All copies" opens one-click chips for every copy. The arrows swap them. Changing the page keeps the pair, changing a copy keeps the page. The switch above the mock-up moves this block between the left panel and a band over the pictures.

    3. 3

      Page list. One row per position in the book. The dot shows how reliable the alignment is for the current pair. Filters: all, to check, flagged (by colour). The whole left panel collapses.

    4. 4

      Book map. A thin line at the bottom edge. Move the cursor there and it opens, showing a small picture of that page in copy 1 and its name. The pin keeps it open. Amber ticks mark pages to check, grey ticks pages one copy lacks, coloured ticks your flags. Pages not prepared yet show no tick.

    5. 5

      Bottom bar. Previous and next page. "Next to check" jumps to the next page where the alignment is uncertain. "Flag page" flags in the standard colour; click it again to pick one of 6 colours or remove the flag.

    6. 6

      Annotations. Circle, rectangle (dotted, movable, resizable) and text note, in the same 6 colours. They belong to the page and show in every pairing; the eye button hides them all. In the mock-up they appear on the superimposed picture only.

    7. 7

      Space for the pictures. Drag the borders between the panels. The arrows button at the top right is focus mode: everything except the pictures disappears, and the keys still work.
