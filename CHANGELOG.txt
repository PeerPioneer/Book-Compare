## [Compare tab: flags and book map] - 2026-11-06
- New: **flags**. The button "Flag page" above the teal bar (or the key F) marks the page you are looking at in the standard colour; click the button again to choose one of six colours (pink-red, coral, yellow, green, blue, dark teal) or to remove the flag. F flags and un-flags. A flag belongs to the page of the book (the same row in every pairing of copies), is saved in the project file and shows as a small flag in the page list of the Compare tab and in the page grid (Pages tab). Changes of flags can be undone in the Pages view (Ctrl+Z)
- Page list: as soon as a page is flagged, the chips "All" and "Flagged N" appear; "Flagged" can be narrowed to one colour
- New: **book map**, a thin line at the bottom edge of the Compare tab, one position per page of the book: grey tick = one of the two copies has no page there, coloured tick = flag, teal mark = the page on screen. Moving the pointer to the line opens it; a small picture of the page (from copy 1, otherwise copy 2) and its name appear while you hover over it; a click jumps to that page (with "Skip pages that one copy lacks" switched on, to the nearest page both copies have). The pin button keeps the map open (remembered); on a touch screen the first tap opens it
- Project file: new field "flags" (row number -> colour 0-5); older projects open without flags, wrong entries are ignored
- tests: test_compare_tab.py now also covers flags (button, colours, F, filter, grid, undo) and the book map (opens, picture, jump, grey ticks, pin); test_workspace.py and test_helper.py check the saved flags
- Still not in the Compare tab (planned): preparing the whole book in advance and saving the results, annotations, "next to check", focus mode, quality dots
